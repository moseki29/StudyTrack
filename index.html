ST StudyTrack

A study journal for busy students

Keep every class, task, and study session in one focused place.

StudyTrack turns scattered notes, deadlines, and study plans into a clear daily agenda so you always know what to do next.

Why StudyTrack matters

Students juggle classes, assignments, and revision across many tools. StudyTrack brings them together, making progress visible and helping you build a steady study rhythm without burning out.

Our mission

Make focused, organized studying accessible to every student by turning daily plans into simple, trackable actions.

How to use it

  1. Add your daily goals, classes, and study sessions.
  2. Review today’s agenda and check items off as you go.
  3. Track weekly progress and streaks by course.
  4. Keep study files and schedules in your private library.

Use StudyTrack on this device

Checking app installation and offline support…

Each device keeps its own planner and file library. To open it on your phone over home Wi-Fi, close the laptop-only launcher and run Run on Wi-Fi for phone.bat, then visit the phone address it prints. Keep the laptop and Wi-Fi on while using that local address.

On iPhone, open the address in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, use the browser menu to install or add it to the Home screen. A local Wi-Fi address is not HTTPS, so offline installation may be limited; full PWA offline caching needs HTTPS or localhost.
